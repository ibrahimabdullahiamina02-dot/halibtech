WHO WE ARE

About HALIB

HALIB Tech & Media Group is a multi-service technology and creative company focused on providing practical digital solutions, creative services, advertising and marketing support, business services and digital education.

We bring technology and creativity together to help individuals, businesses and organizations solve problems, improve their digital presence and turn ideas into practical solutions.

OUR STORY

The Meaning Behind HALIB

HALIB is a name with a meaningful identity behind the brand. The brand also uses the Arabic word حليب, meaning milk, as part of its identity.

HALIB represents a vision of building a company that combines technology, creativity, media, business support and education under one group.

The name HALIB is derived from family Name.
